• ## 1. Executive Summary                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                          
  The audit was read-only. No file, Git reference, artifact, result, or configuration was changed. The repository remained on main at                     
  eb35a9ca49b3b847f9d845edcabd6fe504899cbb with a clean working tree.                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                          
  Key findings:                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                          
  - The repository contains 704 tracked files totaling 1,428,497,746 bytes (1,362.322 MiB).                                                               
  - model/ accounts for 1,248.808 MiB. Most of this is adapter weights and 24 byte-identical tokenizer bundles.                                           
  - Exactly 50 tracked files exceed 5 MiB.                                                                                                                
  - The scientifically valid 7B experiment design is 18 effective combinations: 3 epochs × 3 learning rates × 2 gradient-accumulation settings, with      
    effective dropout fixed at 0.05.                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                          
  - Four nominal d0p0 runs are not valid dropout ablations: their exported adapters report dropout 0.05 and their weights exactly duplicate paired d0p05  
    adapters.                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                          
  - The original TISER 7B baseline and best adapted 7B metrics are intact.                                                                                
  - Canonical loaders, trainers, evaluators, parsers, and fetchers remain unique. Most duplication is in historical orchestration scripts, notebooks, and 
    documentation.                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                          
  - The README, most tennis planning documents, several context-conflict documents, and report/report.tex are materially stale.                           
  - The report references a missing figure and still presents tennis as a 0.5B-only/future experiment.                                                    
  - Reproducibility is limited by missing adapter provenance, missing dataset hashes, machine-specific paths, CLI/notebook-only overrides, and single-seed
    execution.                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                          
  - The checkout could eventually shrink by approximately 1,193.9 MiB while retaining the best adapter, one tokenizer suite, all small scientific         
    metadata, and externally archived unique checkpoints.

  ## 14. Risks and Stop Conditions                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                          
  Stop cleanup if any of the following applies:                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                          
  - An external adapter or dataset has not been re-downloaded and hash-verified.                                                                          
  - The original TISER base adapter cannot be identified by immutable hash/revision.                                                                      
  - A tokenizer consumer requires per-run local files and no pinned fallback has been tested.                                                             
  - The invalid d0p0 metadata or prediction discrepancy would be lost.                                                                                    
  - The 19-line malformed snapshot has not been represented in repair-lineage documentation.                                                              
  - A unique checkpoint would be deleted rather than archived.                                                                                            
  - Dataset licensing does not permit external publication.                                                                                               
  - The missing report figure or its source statistics cannot be recovered.                                                                               
  - Report claims cannot be tied to tracked analysis artifacts.                                                                                           
  - A forgetting/preservation claim is proposed without a general-TISER retention evaluation.                                                             
  - Cleanup would require rewriting published Git history without team coordination.                                                                      
  - Any dropout conclusion treats the nominal d0p0 runs as effective dropout-zero models.                                                                 
  - Any cleanup script uses directory-wide deletion instead of the explicitly verified paths.
